SteamUpdatesCJK Regular and SteamUpdatesCJK Bold are subset fonts derived
from Source Han Sans CN Regular and Source Han Sans CN Bold, Version 2.005.

Source Han Sans copyright 2014-2025 Adobe. The original Source Han Sans font
software is distributed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1; see
OFL-1.1.txt in this directory.

These derivative fonts retain the common simplified Chinese character set,
plugin UI text, common Latin characters, CJK punctuation, fullwidth forms,
and Japanese kana. The derived family was renamed to SteamUpdatesCJK because
"Source" is a Reserved Font Name under the upstream license.
